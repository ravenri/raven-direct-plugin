# Language, Tone and Banned Words

The Raven credit-desk language standard, as it applies to this skill's output.

## Spelling

American: favorable, organization, color, behavior, center, analyze, license, -ize, -or. Convert British spellings in the source documents.

## Register

Measured, precise, declarative. State the position and the risk. Do not sell, and do not characterize where a number will do.

## Do not use

- Sell-side phrasing ("throws off cash", "the story"), "real" as an intensifier, metaphor, rhetorical questions.
- Hype adjectives: best-in-class, robust, compelling, attractive, well-positioned, mission-critical, world-class, market-leading, premier, stellar; "differentiated" as a bare claim.
- "Significant" without a number; "strong" without a benchmark; "proven track record"; "recurring revenue" without the mechanism; "corroborated"; "translates to"; "appropriate" or "comfortable" as vague judgments; "tension"; "key" as filler; "arguably", "quite", "very".
- Em dashes, in text and headings. Use commas, periods, semicolons or colons.
- Platform terms: "the vault", "the tile", "the record", "the family", prompt IDs (Q-numbers), "diligence_summary". Present a finding as a finding; where attribution is needed say "Raven's analysis"; name sources as the underlying documents (deal materials, lender presentation, peer filings).

## Do

- Quantify wherever a figure exists. Name risks plainly. Lead with the conclusion, then the constraint.
- Spell out each acronym on first use.
- Say each fact once. Do not restate in prose what an adjacent table shows.

## Narrative versus row fields

Narrative fields (overview, lead-ins, descriptions) are one to three sentences. Table values are crisp fragments of about 50 characters and no more than 90: lead with the figure or proper noun, no subject-plus-verb preamble, no trailing period, do not repeat the row label, separate sub-facts with a semicolon, drop legal suffixes.

- Weak: "[Sponsor Name] LLC is the sponsor for the acquisition."
- Strong: "[Sponsor Name]".

## Emphasis

Bold key figures (amounts, percentages, multiples, dates) in narrative using double asterisks. No emphasis inside table values and no HTML tags.
