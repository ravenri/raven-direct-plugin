Basket mechanics · reference model

Basket capacity — Project Example (Example Parent, LLC)

Two-tranche structure: executed First Lien and Second Lien Credit Agreements (Apr-16-25), cov-lite for the term loans with a springing First Lien Net Leverage covenant on the revolver only. This model is scoped to the First Lien agreement's own baskets and gates; Second Lien terms are referenced only where they cross-affect First Lien capacity. Figures and section citations are read from the executed agreements, not carried over from any other deal.

Debt incurrence (First Lien)
  • Free & Clear basket: greater of $437.3MM or 100% of Consolidated EBITDA, no test (§2.19 / Maximum Incremental Facilities Amount).
  • Ratio incremental: unlimited if pro forma ≤4.80x First Lien Net Leverage (pari), ≤6.00x Secured Net Leverage (junior lien), or ≤7.00x Total Net Leverage (unsecured) — non-guarantor cap greater of $220.0MM/50% EBITDA.
  • General debt basket: greater of $220.0MM or 50% of Consolidated EBITDA, plus unused Free & Clear capacity (§6.01(bb)).
  • Contribution debt (builder, not modeled as a slider): 200% of net cash proceeds of Qualified Stock/capital contributions, excluding amounts already used under the Cumulative Equity Amount.
  • Cross-agreement debt is a separate raise permission, not summed into this aggregate: up to $525.0MM of Second Lien debt permitted under the First Lien agreement; up to $2.43B of First Lien debt permitted under the Second Lien agreement.
  • Section 1.11 confirms baskets may be accumulated, combined, divided, and later reclassified within the same covenant — not a hypothetical for this deal.
  • MFN: 50bps cap on qualifying floating-rate pari term loans; sunsets 24 months (1L) / 36 months (2L) after closing; excludes up to $220.0MM/50% EBITDA (1L) or $110.0MM/25% EBITDA (2L) of new loans from the test.
Liens (First Lien)
  • General liens basket: greater of $220.0MM or 50% of Consolidated EBITDA, plus unused Free & Clear capacity (§6.02(r)).
  • Identical figure to the general debt basket — sized to fully secure it.
  • Not summed into debt capacity above; a lien permission, not new borrowing.
Investments
  • Ratio: unrestricted at ≤5.50x Total Net Leverage Ratio (§6.04(x)).
  • General basket: greater of $220.0MM or 50% of Consolidated EBITDA, plus unused §6.08(k) dividend capacity, plus the Available Prepayment Capacity Amount (§6.04(w)).
  • Available Prepayment Capacity Amount sizing mechanics are not in the reviewed excerpts — footnoted, not modeled.
  • Non-Guarantor investment cap: greater of $220.0MM/50% EBITDA (§6.04(d)/(f)).
  • Unrestricted Subsidiary investment basket: greater of $154.0MM/35% EBITDA — the agreement's own carve-out, explicitly barred from reclassification in or out (§6.04(bb)).
Distributions & shared reservoirs
  • Restricted payments (dividends): unrestricted at ≤5.00x Total Net Leverage (§6.08(s)).
  • Junior debt payments: unrestricted at the same 5.00x gate (§6.11(a)(vi)) — RP and JDP share one gate.
  • RP general basket: greater of $132.0MM/30% EBITDA (§6.08(k)). JDP general basket: same $132.0MM/30% EBITDA plus unused §6.08(k) capacity (§6.11(a)(v)(1)).
  • Cumulative Amount (the agreement's own term): starter greater of $220.0MM/50% EBITDA; builds from the greater of retained Excess Cash Flow or 50% Consolidated Net Income from Jan 1, 2025. Shared by RP, Investments, and JDP (§6.04(u)(i)/6.08(h)(i)/6.11(a)(iv)).
  • Cumulative Equity Amount: a second, separate reservoir built from post-closing equity proceeds (the functional equivalent of Excluded Contributions). Also shared by RP, Investments, and JDP (§6.04(u)(ii)/6.08(h)(ii)/6.11(a)(iv)(y)).
Documentation notes
  • Financial covenant is springing and revolver-only: 8.10x First Lien Net Leverage Ratio, tested only if Revolving Loans/L/Cs exceed 40.0% of Revolving Commitments (§6.10). Equity cure available: 5 uses over the life of the facility, at least 2 of every 4 fiscal quarters with no cure, capped at the minimum amount required (§8.04).
  • Preferred equity instrument not in the reviewed package — dividend rate, cash-vs-PIK mechanics, and covenant treatment are unconfirmed; potential cash leakage outside the modeled RP baskets.
  • "Excluded Contributions" is not the agreement's defined term here — its functional equivalent is the Cumulative Equity Amount, used per the reservoir description above.

Reading notes

  • Two leverage bases gate different things: First Lien Net Leverage gates the incremental pari/junior-lien/unsecured tiers and the springing covenant; Total Net Leverage gates all three distribution ratios (RP, JDP, Investments). They're modeled with independent sliders here, not derived from one another.
  • Reclassification is confirmed, not hypothetical, on this deal: Section 1.11 states baskets may be accumulated, combined, divided, and reclassified within the same covenant.
  • Two separate reservoirs — Cumulative Amount and Cumulative Equity Amount — both feed all three of RP, Investments, and JDP. They're shown once each as shared pools rather than repeated inside every distribution card, so the model doesn't overstate capacity by counting the same dollar three times.
  • The springing covenant only binds the revolving lenders, and only above 40% revolver utilization — below that threshold, the term loans carry no maintenance test at all, and the 8.10x level itself is never tested.
  • The Unrestricted Subsidiary investment basket ($154MM/35% EBITDA) is the agreement's own exception to its general reclassification rule — explicitly barred from reallocation in or out, unlike every other basket here.
  • Cross-agreement debt capacity ($525MM of 2L debt permitted under the 1L agreement; $2.43B of 1L debt permitted under the 2L agreement) is a separate raise permission tracked in the other agreement, not part of this First Lien aggregate.

Model notes: first lien leverage gates the incremental debt tiers and the springing revolver covenant; total leverage gates RP, junior debt payments, and investments. Distribution capacity below a gate is shown as unrestricted, subject to no default. Figures are drawn from a sample deal's executed First Lien and Second Lien Credit Agreements via Raven's legal-review extraction, except where marked illustrative or flagged as a data gap.
