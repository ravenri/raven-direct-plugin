# Meeting-type guide

1. Management presentation: use Company, Industry and Financial questions only. Collateral & Liquidity and Structural gaps go to Follow-up Requests. Drop market sizing.
2. Expert call: lean on market structure, competitor behavior, pricing and labor. Drop company financials the expert cannot know.
3. Customer or owner reference: lean on service quality, switching and price tolerance. Drop financials and structure.
4. Advisor check-in: lean on adjustments, definitions, add-backs and methodology. Drop operations.

## Rules for spoken wording

1. Start from the vault's own ask.
2. Make it open-ended and neutral.
3. Name one data point per question.
4. Include a figure only if it helps the answer.
5. Drop it if later documents already answer it.

Lead with open-ended questions and let the other side talk, then follow up on specifics. Do not lead the witness. Do not add a closing question unless the user asks for one.
