# Status and date checklist

Run before writing questions. Each hit changes a question or adds a probe.

1. Anything described as pending, planned or upcoming: look in later financial statements, notes, the credit agreement and the model for a completion date.
2. Financing signed after the anchor period.
3. A report marked draft, and whether a final exists.
4. A model or document dated after the QoE or the anchor.
5. The age of the anchor period against today.
6. Peer filings dated after the deal documents.
7. A management claim worded differently in two documents.
8. The same figure carried differently in the vault and a dashboard, or in two vault answers.
9. The meeting date against what the documents call current.
10. Standalone and pro forma figures combined in one ratio.
