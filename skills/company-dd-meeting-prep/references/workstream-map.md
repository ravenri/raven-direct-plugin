# Workstream map

This is a completeness check only. It never adds questions. For each workstream below, confirm that at least one question or follow-up request touches it, or note it in the reply.

1. Earnings quality: the EBITDA bridge, add-backs, adjustments and quality of earnings support. Changes anchor EBITDA.
2. Cash and liquidity: cash flow conversion, capex, working capital, liquidity and fixed-charge coverage. Changes liquidity and coverage.
3. Debt and debt-like items: funded debt, delayed draw and revolver capacity, deferred consideration, earnouts and related-party debt. Changes leverage.
4. Customers and revenue durability: retention, concentration, contract terms, pricing and booking visibility.
5. Suppliers, labor and operations: vendors, labor, cost inflation, systems and integration.
6. Market and competition: market structure, competitors, barriers to entry, pricing power and regulation.
7. Legal, regulatory and other risks: licenses, litigation, environmental, pensions and compliance.
8. Structure and documents: lien ranking, security, covenants, amendments and the credit agreement.
9. Management, acquisitions and plan: management team, acquisition pipeline, forecast and current trading.

## Not covered by the platform

Report as not covered when relevant. Never invent.

1. Financial policy and capital allocation.
2. Counterparty credit quality of customers and suppliers.
3. Customer and supplier reference calls.
4. A schedule of major contracts.
5. The lender group and amendment voting.
6. Leases as a standalone item.
