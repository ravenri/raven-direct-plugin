# Standard phrasings

Use these wordings so runs read the same. Fill the brackets; do not reword the frame.

- Absent figure: "Not disclosed" in a table cell; "[item] is not disclosed" in prose. Name any substitute the company does disclose.
- Two figures that differ: "[figure A] ([source or basis]) against [figure B] ([source or basis]); the basis of the difference is not given." Never choose one silently, never add or subtract to explain the gap.
- Projected or budget figures: mark "(P)" or "(B)" in the sentence; never in a historical table.
- Interview or third-party evidence: "(an interview sample, not a company metric)".
- Disclosure limit (only where lender materials assert the opposite in words without figures): "[claim] is asserted without figures."
- Credit implication: starts with the label "Credit implication." in the document; one or two sentences, written from the facts above it, quantified where a figure exists.
- Acronyms: spell out on first use, in the sentence where it first appears. The check script lists the ones it enforces.
- Never write: the vault, the tile, the answers, the analysis, stored, prompt IDs, Raven.
