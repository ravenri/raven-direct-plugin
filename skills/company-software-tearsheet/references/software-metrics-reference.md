# Software metrics reference

The only permitted source of formulas and reference ranges in the tearsheet. Ranges are context, not pass or fail:
never present one as a verdict, a rating or a benchmark result. Status: approved by the owner on October 15, 2026, with the billings growth range dropped.

| Metric | Formula | Reference range | Source and date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue line analysis | Split of revenue between subscription, term and perpetual license, maintenance, services and consumption | More subscription and maintenance than perpetual license | Onex Falcon software white paper (Barclays U.S. Software Coverage), 2021 | Qualitative |
| ARR | Annual recurring revenue as stated | None | none | Report the company's definition and period |
| Billings | Revenue plus change in deferred revenue | None | none | Range dropped by the owner: a 2021 listed-company average |
| Bookings | Revenue plus change in remaining performance obligation | Leading indicator | Onex Falcon white paper (Barclays), 2021 | Qualitative |
| Professional services mix | Professional services as a share of total revenue | No greater than 20 to 25% | Onex Falcon white paper (Barclays), 2021 | Refresh |
| Gross retention | (Beginning customers less customers lost) / beginning customers | 90%+ | Onex Falcon white paper; stored scorecard band | |
| Customer churn | 1 less gross retention | No more than 10% | Onex Falcon white paper | |
| Net dollar retention | (Beginning revenue + up-sells less down-sells less churn) / beginning revenue | Above 100%. ARR band $50MM to $200MM: 110%+ strong. ARR band $200MM+: 115%+ strong | Onex Falcon white paper; stored scorecard bands | The paper's 115% industry average was later corrected to about 101% for private companies; do not use it |
| ARR up for renewal | ARR with renewals due in the next twelve months | None | none | |
| Client concentration | Revenue from the top clients as a share of total revenue | Top 10 under 30% preferred | Stored scorecard band | State when the company reports a different basis |
| SMB exposure | SMB revenue as a share of total revenue | SMB clients carry higher risk | Onex Falcon white paper | Qualitative |
| Gross margin | (Revenue less cost of revenue) / revenue | 80%+ for subscription; about 75% blended | Onex Falcon white paper (Barclays), 2021; stored scorecard band | Hybrid businesses read lower |
| Adjusted EBITDA margin | Adjusted EBITDA / revenue | None | none | |
| Rule of 40 | Revenue growth plus free cash flow margin | Above 40 | Onex Falcon white paper; stored scorecard band | Name the substitution when EBITDA margin replaces free cash flow |
| LTV to CAC | Gross margin / gross dollar churn, over the CAC ratio | 3x minimum; 5x healthy | Stored scorecard band | Not the white paper formula |
| CAC payback | Sales and marketing / (new ARR times gross margin) times 12 | 24 months or less ($50MM to $200MM ARR); 18 months or less ($200MM+ ARR) | Stored scorecard bands | |
| Magic number | Change in annualized revenue / prior-quarter sales and marketing | Above 0.75 efficient; 0.5 to 0.75 caution | Onex Falcon white paper (Barclays), 2021 | |
| Cash conversion | (Adjusted EBITDA less capex) / adjusted EBITDA | None | none | Follow the stored conversion definition |

ARR growth: 40 to 60% ($50MM to $200MM ARR), 20 to 40% ($200MM+ ARR), shown only when ARR growth is stored and ARR
falls in the band.
